Security Jelly 1

Same skills, different permission. Crack passwords, catch bots, trap a sneaky program and lock your files, all on a pretend computer. Seven missions.

The full rich interactivity and progress tracking is currently only available on desktop: a laptop or desktop computer, with the window at least 900 × 560.

What you will learn: 7 lessons

  1. hatsWhat is the difference between hacking and breaking the law?
  2. chmodWho can read my files, and how do I lock them properly?
  3. guessHow do attackers crack passwords, and what makes one strong?
  4. botsWho is trying to log in to my computer at 3 in the morning?
  5. roomIs that "free coins" download safe to run?
  6. layersOne of my defences failed. Am I done for?
  7. greyI found a way in by accident. What should I do?

    Think like a hacker. Build like a defender.

    Security people wear two hats: one to find the way in, one to lock it. Learn both, safely, on a pretend computer: passwords, bots, sneaky programs and locks. Part 1 has seven missions.
