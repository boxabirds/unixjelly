Overview

Follow one command, ls, from your fingers down to the disk and back. Press Next. Drag to turn the house, scroll to zoom, double-click to straighten up.

Prototype

What's different from "The whole game"
  • Called "Overview", which needs no explaining.
  • One house, cut open, instead of floating islands: each layer is a floor, so nothing pokes into the floor above, and every stop fills the frame.
  • Your letters travel as jelly. The l and s you type pop out of the screen and ride a lift down. The answer comes back up as jelly words, so you can see what the kernel sends back.
  • The file tree is the house's roots, so "a tree that grows upside down" is something you can see.
  • King Kernel has a face, not a screen, so he can't be mistaken for the terminal. Shelly gets a real spiral shell.
  • The ls program waits in the /bin cupboard on Shelly's floor.
