What's different from the old Backstage
  • The same house as the Overview, so Backstage zooms into a place you've already seen, instead of a new flat drawing.
  • Your typed line arrives as jelly words, and Shelly snips it at the spaces. The program word turns pink and the arguments blue, with little tags.
  • The program is a character. The ls and mv robots and the cat step out of the /bin cupboard, holding the arguments over their heads.
  • The lift replaces the glass tube, and King Kernel gives permission on his own floor.
  • The answer is a card, not a thin see-through rectangle: it shows the lines that will appear on the screen. mv changes the hamster's label in the roots and carries nothing back.
